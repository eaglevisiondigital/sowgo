Discipleship & follow-up

What Happens
After Salvation?

Many people ask, “What happens to the people who get saved?” Our heart is to help every person who responds to Jesus take their next step—not leave them at their first step.

We invite new believers into a 13-week discipleship journey and encourage connection with the local church involved in the outreach. Our connected systems are being prepared to help track progress, support follow-up, and keep people growing in their faith.

About this review website

This is the approved-design implementation for the standalone SowGo website. Photographs are illustrative placeholders, not documentary ministry images. Funding bars show a zeroed design state and are not financial records. App access, connected progress tracking, and direct shared-backend features are not launched here.

Registration, partnership, prayer, discipleship, and sowing links lead to the existing Champion Life pathways. This website does not collect personal information, create registrations, process payments, or change the recipient of a gift. No new Supabase project or database has been created. Event dates and links are a read-only snapshot of the existing outreach page; venue information and pending registrations remain subject to confirmation.
